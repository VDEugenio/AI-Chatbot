# How Vaughn Sells

## His core strength: making the technology simple and making the connection

Vaughn's biggest strength in sales is **building a real connection with prospects** and **explaining technical solutions simply**, so the value is obvious to everyone in the room. This comes up in almost every deal cycle he's part of.

He works on being able to **speak to anyone in the room, whatever their background**: the C-suite, sales leaders and producers, operations teams, and the customer's technical team. His approach follows a consistent sequence:

1. **Relate** to the person and where they're coming from.
2. **Understand** their actual problems by asking real discovery questions instead of pitching.
3. **Acknowledge** those problems, so they feel heard.
4. **Break down** how the solution creates value for *them* specifically, in their own terms.

His engineering background is what makes this work. Vaughn has built distributed systems professionally, so he can go deep with a technical evaluator and then turn the same idea into plain business value for an executive in the next sentence.

## Discovery: MEDDPICC

Vaughn is a big believer in **MEDDPICC** (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition). He uses it to run discovery and qualify deals, especially the deals he runs end-to-end as the AE. It gives a 4–8 month enterprise cycle structure: who signs, what they measure success by, how and when the decision gets made, who is championing the deal internally, and what the alternatives are.

## Making a strong impression

Throughout a sales cycle, Vaughn makes sure prospects see four things in him:

- **Competent:** he knows the product, the insurance workflows, and the technology.
- **Hard-working:** fast follow-ups, and demos built specifically for them.
- **Reliable:** he does what he says he'll do, when he says he'll do it.
- **Easy to work with:** a buying process with him should feel collaborative, not adversarial.

## Rapid, custom demos and proofs of concept

Much of Vaughn's day is **discovery calls** and **building custom demos and proofs of concept**. He has **built demos from scratch in under a week** several times, and every one of those prospects is still actively engaged.

At a startup, prospects sometimes want a capability that isn't built yet. Vaughn **sells ahead of the roadmap** by building the demo himself. He did this for a **certificate of insurance (COI) workflow** and an **Employee Benefits workflow** (see feathery_deal_stories.md).

## Land and expand

Vaughn often leads with Feathery's **quote generation**, **proposal generation**, and **Employee Benefits guide generation** tools. They're affordable, quick to implement, and show what the platform can do. Once a customer sees value from one workflow, it opens the door to larger upsells across the platform.

## Technical depth as a sales advantage

Vaughn **writes code** and configures **API integrations** and connections to customers' existing systems, such as **agency management systems (AMS)** and **CRMs**. He doesn't need to hand technical questions off. He can answer them in the room, build the integration for the proof of concept, and stay credible with the customer's IT and engineering teams.

## Using AI on both sides of the house

Vaughn keeps sharpening his **AI skills** to be more effective in two ways:

- **Selling and understanding customers:** using AI to research accounts and stakeholders, so he walks into every conversation already knowing who they are and what they're likely to need.
- **Building demos fast:** using AI-assisted development to deliver strong, tailored demos on very short timelines.

He also built his own AI-powered prospecting tool, ICP Lab (see icp_lab.md), which he has also used for outbound prospecting at Feathery.
