# Sales Journey, Mentorship, and Career Direction

## Roles Vaughn is interested in

Vaughn is interested in **Solutions Engineer** and **Account Executive** roles.

He brings both halves of what those roles need. He has the **technical skills and technical experience**: 3+ years as a software engineer at DraftKings and SRC, and he still writes code and builds integrations today. He also has the **drive, persona, and charisma** for sales, which he's now proving in a quota-carrying role at Feathery.

## Why sales

Sales has fascinated Vaughn since he was a kid. His **dad has been in sales successfully for over 40 years**, selling specialty paper. Growing up, Vaughn watched what it took: the relationships, the persistence, the wins and the losses.

His dad is still one of his biggest role models and a mentor in sales. He's an encyclopedia of sales knowledge. The two of them have frequent conversations where his dad shares lessons from his wins, his losses, and the different phases of his career, and Vaughn soaks up as much as he can. Specialty paper and insurance software are very different products sold to very different buyers. But Vaughn finds that the **underlying principles of sales stay the same**: trust, understanding the customer's real problem, follow-through, and persistence.

Vaughn's engineering career already pointed this way. At SRC and DraftKings he kept gravitating toward the human-facing parts of the job: demos to customers, on-site visits, translating technical work for leadership, and pitching solutions across teams. Moving into sales was a deliberate choice to put that at the center of his career.

## The career switch and his "SDR phase"

In 2026 Vaughn made a deliberate switch from software engineering into a sales-forward role. After his first round of applications, he realized he would have to really dig in and learn the sales process from the ground up. He did it two ways:

**1. Learning from people.** Vaughn did extensive LinkedIn outreach and found peers in sales and solutions roles who mentored him. Many started out as essentially strangers but were willing to get on a call and share their insight and experience in the industry. He is very thankful for that generosity, and it shapes how he wants to pay it forward.

**2. Learning by building, the way he was trained in school.** Vaughn built his own outreach CRM to track every message and how well it performed, and then turned it into **ICP Lab**, a tool that predicts who at a target company is most likely to respond (see icp_lab.md).

That period worked like his **SDR/BDR phase**: real, high-volume cold outreach.

- He sent **over 2,000 outreach messages** across **LinkedIn, cold email, and cold calling**.
- **LinkedIn outreach was the most effective channel by a long shot.**
- He ran **formal A/B tests** on his message openers and kept the version that performed best.
- From the outreach he tracked, about **1 in 4 recipients clicked through** to his portfolio, **12–18% replied**, and he booked **30 calls**. People who clicked his link were **3.8× more likely to reply** than people who didn't.

That hands-on prospecting experience carries straight into his business development work at Feathery.

## What makes someone great at sales, and Vaughn's evidence

Research on top-performing B2B salespeople keeps pointing to the same traits. Gong's analysis of recorded sales calls, Harvard Business Review's study of top sellers' personality traits, and surveys of sales leaders all highlight these:

| Trait of top sellers | Vaughn's evidence |
|---|---|
| **Listening more than talking; asking problem-focused questions throughout discovery** | Runs discovery on MEDDPICC; his approach starts with relating, understanding, and acknowledging the prospect's problem before presenting anything. |
| **Being knowledgeable and credible** (buyers' most common word for the best reps) | A former software engineer who writes code and builds API/AMS/CRM integrations himself, so he can answer technical questions in the room. |
| **Customer focus** | Builds custom demos for each prospect, sometimes for workflows that don't exist yet (COI, Employee Benefits). |
| **Conscientiousness and reliability** | Built demos from scratch in under a week several times; prioritizes being reliable and easy to work with throughout a cycle. |
| **Humility, and putting the team at the center** | Saved an at-risk account by openly owning a platform regression; credits his mentors and his dad. |
| **Drive, discipline, and self-motivation** | 2,000+ outreach messages during his career switch; on pace for ~140% of a $750K ARR quota; asked to run full deal cycles as the AE to grow. |
| **Resilience: not taking rejection personally** | Cold outreach at volume across LinkedIn, email, and phone; learned from a tough first round of applications and doubled down. |
| **Coachability and continuous learning** | Ongoing mentorship from his dad and Feathery colleagues; works every day on sales skills, demos, messaging, and charisma. |
| **Using data and tools to sell smarter** | Built ICP Lab to score prospects from his own outreach data; uses AI for account research and rapid demo building. |

## Honest about experience, confident about direction

Vaughn is open about it: he **doesn't have decades of sales experience**. But he is **highly motivated to perfect the craft every day**, working on his sales ability, demo skills, messaging, and charisma. In his short time at Feathery he has already **proven to himself and to the company that sales is where he is most effective**: on pace for ~140% of quota, the top conference meeting-booker among the SEs, and trusted to run full deal cycles as the AE.

He is **always willing to learn** and **always willing to mentor**, paying forward the help he got from peers who took his calls when he was starting out. He is also **confident in his current skill set and in where he's headed**: a long-term career in technical sales, where engineering depth and genuine human connection together are his edge.
