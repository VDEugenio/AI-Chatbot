# Feathery — Solutions Engineer (July 2026 – Present)

**Vaughn's current job:** Vaughn Eugenio is a **Solutions Engineer at Feathery**, on the **insurance** team. He joined in **July 2026**, shortly after Feathery's Series A. It is a **quota-carrying** sales role. He works **remotely from North Charleston, SC**, and travels for industry conferences and for customer and prospect onsites.

## About Feathery

Feathery is a venture-backed startup that describes itself as an **AI operating and decisioning system for financial services**. It was founded in 2021 by Peter Dun and Zack Khan (both Forbes 30 Under 30). The company is often listed as San Francisco–based; its office is in Toronto, Canada. Feathery began as a no-code form builder and grew into a platform with two layers: one that collects and structures data flowing through a business's workflows, and an AI decisioning layer that analyzes that data and surfaces insights and recommendations.

- **Funding:** In July 2026 Feathery announced its **Series A, led by Portage Ventures**, bringing total funding to roughly **$30M**. The funds go toward AI products built on data shared across its client base, and toward growing engineering and go-to-market teams.
- **Customers:** 300+ firms across **insurance** and **wealth management**.

### What Feathery sells to insurance

Vaughn sells to both sides of the insurance market:

- **Carriers and MGAs:** underwriting and submission intake, automated parsing of ACORD forms and loss runs, and faster quote turnaround.
- **Brokers:** auto-filling carrier applications and portals from ACORD data, pulling data out of PDFs, spreadsheets, and agency management systems (AMS), comparing quotes across carriers, generating client proposals, and e-signature and approval flows.
- Feathery covers both **P&C (property & casualty)** and **Employee Benefits**, which sets it apart from P&C-only competitors.

The products Vaughn sells most often are Feathery's **quote generation / quote comparison**, **proposal generation**, and **Employee Benefits guide generation** tools. They're affordable, they can be implemented quickly, and they show what the platform can do. That makes them a strong foot in the door, and they open the way to larger expansions later (land-and-expand).

## Scope of the role

- **Pre-sale and post-sale, with a heavy pre-sale focus.** Pre-sales is where Vaughn is most effective and what he enjoys most: discovery calls, tailored demos, proofs of concept, and technical validation.
- **Post-sale:** He has supported implementation and post-sale success for **5+ accounts, each worth several hundred thousand dollars**.
- **Wearing many hats:** Feathery is a startup, so Vaughn's work goes beyond a typical SE role. He also does **business development** (sourcing prospects at conferences) and has **run deals end-to-end as the Account Executive** for certain customers. He has owned everything from the first conversation through discovery, demo build, and into contract negotiation.
- **Technical work:** Vaughn writes code and configures integrations himself: API integrations and connections to customers' existing systems such as AMS platforms and CRMs.
